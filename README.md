# My Favorite Series
 
## The Summer I Turned Pretty
 
### About the Series
 
**The Summer I Turned Pretty** is a romantic
drama series about a girl named Isabel
"Belly" Conklin. The story focuses on her
summer experiences, friendship, family,
and love.
 
### Why I Like This Series
 
I like this series because it shows different
feelings about love, growing up, and making
decisions. I also enjoy the story because
it is emotional and interesting.
 
### My Favorite Characters
 
- Belly Conklin
- Conrad Fisher
- Jeremiah Fisher
- Steven Conklin
- Taylor Jewel
 
### What I Like About It
 
1. The romantic story
2. The summer setting
3. The friendship and family moments
4. The emotional scenes
5. The music used in the series
 
### My Favorite Quote
 
> Some summers change everything.
 
### My Rating
 
**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5**
 
### Conclusion
 
This series is one of my favorites because
it reminds me that growing up comes with
happiness, heartbreak, and unforgettable
memories. It is a series worth watching.
